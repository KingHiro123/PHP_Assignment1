1. This large mammal has a long trunk and big ears, and is known for its intelligence and memory.
Answer: Elephant

2. This black-and-white bear-like animal is native to China and primarily eats bamboo.
Answer: Panda

3. Known for its colorful feathers and ability to mimic human speech, this bird is often kept as a pet.
Answer: Parrot

4. This animal is the fastest land mammal, capable of reaching speeds over 100 km/h.
Answer: Cheetah

5. This marine animal has eight arms and is famous for its intelligence and ability to camouflage.
Answer: Octopus

6. This flightless bird is native to Antarctica and is excellent at swimming.
Answer: Penguin

7. Known for its pouch, this Australian marsupial carries its young in it.
Answer: Kangaroo

8. This large feline is called the “king of the jungle” and lives mainly in African savannas.
Answer: Lion
